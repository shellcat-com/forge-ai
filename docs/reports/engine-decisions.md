Candidate starting allocation: one 2-vCPU/4-GiB build and one 1-vCPU/2-GiB preview, on hardware with additional measured capacity for host, DB, collector and external harness. Begin at one executing job, not the RFC's unmeasured ten-job proposal. Pin Firecracker/jailer/kernel/rootfs, disable guest internet, use distinct runtime identities, and enforce quotas plus independent watchdogs. Firecracker requires KVM, maintained host/guest kernels and jailer-equivalent confinement; installing the binary does not establish isolation. [Primary host guidance](https://github.com/firecracker-microvm/firecracker/blob/main/docs/prod-host-setup.md).
