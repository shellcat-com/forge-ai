# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Forge AI a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject contributions that are inconsistent with this Code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers' contact method. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
